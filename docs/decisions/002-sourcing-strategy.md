# Decision 002 — Sourcing Strategy

## Initial sources

We will start with two sources:

1. Y Combinator
2. Hacker News

## Rationale

Y Combinator provides a relatively structured discovery surface for
early-stage startups, including company descriptions, founders, websites,
and batch information.

Hacker News provides a complementary, community-driven signal around
technical launches, developer interest, and recent activity.

Using two sources allows us to go deeper on each source instead of
building a broad but shallow multi-source scraper.

## Intended flow

YC will primarily be used for startup discovery.

Hacker News will primarily be used for freshness and traction enrichment.

The sourcing stage should produce 10–20 qualified startup candidates.

## Rejected approach

We are intentionally not starting with a large number of sources such as
Product Hunt, X/Twitter, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, Reddit, and others.

The goal is depth, reproducibility, and traceability rather than maximum
source coverage.
