# Decision 003 — Candidate Qualification

## Core criteria

A startup is considered relevant to the thesis when:

1. AI is central to the product.
2. The product executes a meaningful business workflow rather than
   merely generating content or providing conversational assistance.
3. The workflow has meaningful economic value through revenue generation,
   cost reduction, time savings, or operational efficiency.

## Autonomy

We do not require zero human involvement.

A workflow can still qualify when humans retain approval or exception
handling, provided the AI system owns most of the workflow execution.

## Strong examples

- Autonomous sales development
- Customer support agents resolving tickets
- AI systems handling procurement workflows
- AI financial/reconciliation agents
- AI agents executing software engineering workflows

## Weak examples

- Generic chatbots
- AI writing assistants
- Copilots where the human performs most of the work
- Thin wrappers around foundation models
- Traditional SaaS products with an AI feature

## Reason for this boundary

The thesis is intended to identify companies where increasing AI
capability can directly expand the amount of economically valuable work
performed by the product.
