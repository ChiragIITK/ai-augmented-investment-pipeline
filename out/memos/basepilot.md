## Team

Two-person founding team with strong technical and startup pedigree. Ken Hendricks (CEO) previously led product at a B2B fintech that grew to 3M users in under 12 months with customers like Visa and Bank of America, and earlier built a computer vision startup to $250K ARR with partners including Deloitte, KPMG, EY and BMW (Founder bio: Ken Hendricks). The team blends product/commercial experience with deep AI/ML research depth.

## Product

Basepilot builds line-of-business-specific AI agents for insurance claims operations, targeting insurers, TPAs and MGAs to make claims processing more efficient, less costly and more intelligent (YC long description, One-liner). The founders describe the product as 'AI coworkers that collaborate with humans to automate work' (Founder bio: Ken Hendricks). YC tags include AI Assistant, No-code and Automation, suggesting a human-in-the-loop assistive framing rather than fully autonomous execution (YC industries/tags).

## Risks & open questions

- Workflow framing is mixed: YC tags 'AI Assistant' and the founders describe 'AI coworkers that collaborate with humans,' suggesting an assistive tool rather than an agent that autonomously owns the claims workflow (YC industries/tags; Founder bio: Ken Hendricks).
- No explicit autonomy language in the sources — unclear how much of the claims workflow the agents actually execute end-to-end versus assist (Score breakdown: Autonomy 0/15).
- Company is tiny (team size 2) and no momentum signals (hiring, traction metrics, revenue) are available; depth of the named Old Republic/Zurich relationships is unspecified (YC team size; YC long description).
- Insurance claims is a regulated, incumbent-heavy domain; competitive landscape and defensibility are entirely absent from the sources.

## What would change our mind

- Evidence that Basepilot's agents autonomously own and execute a high-value claims workflow end-to-end (e.g. adjudication or settlement) rather than assisting humans.
- Traction proof from the named customers — production deployments, contract value, or volume of claims processed at Old Republic International and Zurich (YC long description).
- Momentum signals such as active hiring, revenue growth, or expansion beyond claims into broader autonomous operations.
