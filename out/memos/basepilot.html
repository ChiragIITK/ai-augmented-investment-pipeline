With no momentum data and only limited freshness credit, the venture does not currently fit the autonomous-agent thesis, though evidence of true end-to-end autonomy could warrant a re-look.

Team

Two-person founding team with strong technical and startup pedigree. Ken Hendricks (CEO) previously led product at a B2B fintech that grew to 3M users in under 12 months with customers like Visa and Bank of America, and earlier built a computer vision startup to $250K ARR with partners including Deloitte, KPMG, EY and BMW (Founder bio: Ken Hendricks). Pascal Wieler (CTO) holds a CS MSc from CMU MLD and KIT, co-founded a robotics startup, did research in robotic manipulation and imitation learning at Carnegie Mellon, and worked on self-driving cars at Mercedes-Benz (Founder bio: Pascal Wieler). The team blends product/commercial experience with deep AI/ML research depth.

Product

Basepilot builds line-of-business-specific AI agents for insurance claims operations, targeting insurers, TPAs and MGAs to make claims processing more efficient, less costly and more intelligent (YC long description, One-liner). The founders describe the product as 'AI coworkers that collaborate with humans to automate work' (Founder bio: Ken Hendricks). YC tags include AI Assistant, No-code and Automation, suggesting a human-in-the-loop assistive framing rather than fully autonomous execution (YC industries/tags).

The target market is insurance claims operations serving insurers, TPAs and MGAs (YC long description). The sources name Old Republic International and Zurich among customers using Basepilot (YC long description).
