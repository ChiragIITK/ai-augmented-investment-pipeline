Product

Veritus is an omni-channel platform that deploys AI agents for loan servicing and collections in consumer lending, including inbound and outbound voice agents. The agents negotiate repayment plans, follow up across channels, and drive recoveries with, per the company, zero human involvement. The pitch is that combining voice AI with regulatory compliance and vertical specialization reduces cost-to-collect while increasing recovery rates.

Market

The target market is the consumer lending industry, specifically loan servicing and collections. No market size, customer count, revenue, pricing, or named competitors are provided in the sources, so the size and competitive landscape are unknown. The why-now is implicit in the founders' framing around 'cutting edge voice AI' but is not quantified. Regulatory compliance in collections is called out as a core capability but no specifics (jurisdictions, licensing) are given.

Risks & open questions

  • Collections is a heavily regulated activity; the sources assert 'deep regulatory compliance' but provide no evidence of licensing, legal review, or how autonomous agents stay within debt-collection regulations — a compliance failure could be existential.
  • 'Zero human involvement' voice agents negotiating with distressed consumers carries reputational and consumer-protection risk that is unaddressed in the sources.
  • No traction, revenue, customer, or recovery-rate evidence is provided, so the core claims of lower cost-to-collect and higher recoveries are unverified.
  • Competitive landscape and differentiation versus other collections/voice-AI vendors are entirely absent from the sources.

What would change our mind

  • Evidence of live lender customers and measurable results on recovery rate and cost-to-collect.
  • A credible, documented regulatory/compliance approach (licensing, legal sign-off) for autonomous collections across relevant jurisdictions.
  • Signals of technical depth in voice AI (in-house models/engineering) rather than reliance on generic tooling.
