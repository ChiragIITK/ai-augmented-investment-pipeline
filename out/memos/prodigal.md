- Autonomy is unclear: the product is described as "AI agents" but the sources give no evidence of how autonomously they actually operate versus assisting human teams, which is central to the thesis.
- Collections and loan servicing are heavily regulated (consumer finance); compliance and legal exposure around automated debtor contact could constrain product scope, though the sources say nothing about how this is handled.
- No market sizing, customer, revenue, or competitive data is provided, making it impossible to assess traction or defensibility from the sources.
- Founder backgrounds are opaque — no prior experience, technical depth, or domain credibility is documented in the sources.

## What would change our mind

- Concrete traction data — named lenders as customers, revenue, or payment-volume outcomes — that the sources currently lack.
- Founder or team track record demonstrating deep fintech or AI-research credibility beyond the general claim in the company description.
