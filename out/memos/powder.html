Recommendation — Watch

Watch is appropriate: the team is a clear strength (10/10) with deep wealth-management domain expertise and a prior Addepar exit (Founder bios), but the product's alignment with the autonomous-agent thesis is only partial — execution language mixed with assistive 'copilot'/'insights' framing and no explicit autonomy (Score breakdown: Workflow ownership 17/30, Autonomy 0/15). With no traction or market data in the sources and a modest overall score of 51/100, the case merits monitoring rather than immediate investment pending proof of autonomy and commercial traction.

The team combines technical depth, prior startup/exit experience, and deep wealth-management domain knowledge.

Product

Powder is an AI tool that helps wealth advisors rapidly create personalized sales proposals for prospective clients (YC long description). It uses LLMs to automate manual tasks across three features: a brokerage/tax/estate document parser, a meeting notetaker that captures personalized insights, and a portfolio analysis tool that optimizes returns, risk and fees (YC long description). The stated value is saving hours and producing sharp proposals that help firms win new business (YC long description). In practice it reads as an AI sales copilot for wealth advisors rather than a fully autonomous agent (Founder bio: Soo Yung Cho).
