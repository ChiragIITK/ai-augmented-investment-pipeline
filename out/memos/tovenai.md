## Product

TovenAI builds a platform of AI agents that automate manual, data-intensive work for compliance teams at institutional trading firms, with six agents currently live (YC long description). It ships prebuilt connectors to more than 30 systems—including Bloomberg, Cloud9, and NICE Actimize—so firms can onboard and evaluate results on their own data in under a week (YC long description). They report customers ranging from specialist firms to large global institutions, with agents supporting multiple compliance workflows at a top-10 institutional brokerage (YC long description).
